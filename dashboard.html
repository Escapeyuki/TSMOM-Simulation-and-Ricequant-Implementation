Time series momentum, out of sample

Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012), Time series momentum, Journal of Financial Economics 104, 228–250, re-run on China futures via Ricequant and on global futures via two independent vendors. Every number on this page comes from one run of export_dashboard.py, which refuses to write its output unless it reproduces the repository's published results exactly.

Futures returns are excess returns by construction and stay in local currency; no CNY/USD conversion is applied. All results are gross of transaction costs except where the frictions panel says otherwise.
